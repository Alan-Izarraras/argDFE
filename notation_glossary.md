#Glosario.md

$ s $               Selection coefficient of a derived allele.

$ D $               A Demographic scenario.

$ N $               Effective population size in a generation.

$N_{0} $            Effective population size in most ancient epoch on a demographic scenario.

$ \gamma = 2N_{0}s   $  Strenght of natural selection.

$ z $       A particular \gamma value 

$t_{k}$     A discrete time point.

$g$     A local genealogy with an ancestral and derived allele.

$ K $       A set of discrete $t_{k}$ time points.

$ G $       A set of _g_ local genealogies.

$ G^{count} $       A count of _g_ local genealogies.

$C_{gk}$       The number of lineages containing a derived allele at timepoint _k_ and genealogy _g_ .

$ S $       The number of observed segregating sites.

$ l $       Total number of observed sites (target size).

$ \mu $     Mutation rate per site per generation.

$ \theta = 4N\mu l $    The population scaled mutation rate (genetic diversity metric).

$ w $       A grouping of derived allele frequency values.

$ n $       number of sampled chromosomes in a population.

$F^{w}$     a group from a predefined set of a number W of groups that jointly have all the possible Cgk values from _0_ to _n-1_, where _n_ is the sample size in terms of sampled chromosomes.

$ j $

$m(D,\gamma) $ Number of mutation segregating at present time in a Demographic scenario _D_ evolving with a scaled selection parameter _\gamma_ .

$ e $   Laplace smoothing constant  ($e=0.001$).

$  \Psi $   A distribution of fitness effects.

$\alpha$  Shape parameter of a gamma distribution.

$\beta$     Scale parameter of a gamma distribution.

$ E $       Expected value.
